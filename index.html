Welcome

Quality Childcare That Builds Foundations

At New Beginning, we recognise the profound impact that quality creche services have on a child's early development. Our creches in Pretoria deliver outstanding childcare with a focus on infant care, early learning and comprehensive child development.

With personalised attention and low child-to-educator ratios, children gain confidence, adaptability and an eagerness for discovery — transitioning smoothly to more advanced stages of learning.

  • Nurturing, safe and loving environment
  • Low child-to-educator ratios
  • Holistic growth — cognitive, emotional & social
  • Simplified, supportive registration process
